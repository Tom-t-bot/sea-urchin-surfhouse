How to get there?

The best way to get to our surf house is from Lisbon Airport, which is just a 40-minute drive from Ericeira.
Ericeira is one of Portugal's most popular beaches, not only because of the beauty and quality of the waves but also because of the quick access it offers.
Our Surf House (bungalow and loft) is located 3 minutes from Coxos Beach, and the Ribeira d'ilhas apartment, on the other hand, is located close to the center of Ericeira and, of course, facing Ribeira d'Ilhas Beach.
