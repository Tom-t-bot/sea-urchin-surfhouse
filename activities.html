Activities

Check It

  • Surf Guide

    One of our main services is the Surf Guide. We know you're here to surf the best waves in the region, and we're ready to take you there. Our local guides know the best spots, are familiar with the ideal wind, tide and swell conditions and will make sure you're in the right place at the right time. There's nothing better than having an experienced local by your side to ensure the best surf sessions.

  • Yoga

    We also offer packages with yoga classes. Surfing and yoga complement each other perfectly, providing balance, flexibility and muscle strengthening. Our qualified instructors will guide you through yoga sessions specially designed for surfers, helping you to connect with your body, mind and spirit, and improve your performance on the waves.

  • Airport transfers

    We also make it easy for you to get to us with our airport transfer service. We know that traveling can be tiring, and we want to make your arrival as smooth as possible. Our staff will be waiting for you at the airport to take you directly to our surf House, so you can relax and start your adventure as soon as possible.

At our Surf House, we offer much more than just comfortable accommodation and a relaxed atmosphere. We also offer a complete surfing experience, with additional services that will take your stay to the next level.

Please note: All our activities are subject to availability and require prior scheduling, which must be done directly via WhatsApp.
